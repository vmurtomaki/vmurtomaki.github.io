P02

Adaptive Conformal Forecasting

View repo
PythonMAPIE DockerPlotly uvPytest

Delivers mathematically guaranteed prediction intervals for volatile energy demand series, replacing point forecasts with actionable uncertainty bands that cut over-provisioning costs by [Insert % metric].

  • Implemented EnbPI with block-bootstrap residuals to preserve chronological exchangeability where standard conformal methods break down on time series.
  • Corrected a coarse-grained Adaptive Conformal Inference update to a per-timestep Gibbs & Candes formulation, restoring true reactivity to regime shifts and shocks.
  • Containerized the full inference stack with Docker and validated it end-to-end via an automated smoke-test script covering builds, health checks, and live serving.
P03

GARCH-EVT Risk Engine

View repo
Pythonarch SciPyuvPytest

Forecasts Value-at-Risk and Expected Shortfall for Nordic day-ahead electricity prices under negative-price regimes, giving risk desks a statistically defensible early-warning system worth [Insert % metric] in avoided capital misallocation.

  • Fitted a GJR-GARCH(1,1,1) volatility filter with Student-t innovations, resolving an optimizer underflow bug that silently broke convergence at realistic price scales.
  • Implemented a Peaks-Over-Threshold GARCH-EVT pipeline (McNeil & Frey) with rolling out-of-sample validation via Kupiec, Christoffersen, and Acerbi-Szekely tests.
  • Diagnosed and fixed a zero-violation Kupiec test bypass and removed a statistically indefensible pass/fail heuristic, replacing both with closed-form, peer-reviewed test statistics.
